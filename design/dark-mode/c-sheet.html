True black vs espresso-black on an LCD iPad (approximation)

An LCD can't switch pixels off: the backlight leaks through, so #000 shows up as roughly #0f0f0f, a flat, slightly cool grey with a brighter glow toward the edges. The simulation lifts every value with a screen blend of #0f0f11 plus edge bleed. True black (C2) loses its "void" and turns into neutral charcoal that fights the warm text. Espresso-black (C3) lifts to a warm dark brown, so it still reads as intended. On OLED, C3 is visibly brown-black rather than black, but it stays deep and saturated.
